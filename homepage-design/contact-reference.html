Questions

Before you send.

How long does a project take?

A starter website takes 2 to 3 weeks and a professional one 3 to 6. A ShopNex store takes 4 to 14 weeks depending on the plan. We confirm the timeline in writing before we start.

What is included in a website project?

Design, development, testing, deployment help and a documented handover, plus a support period of 1 to 2 months depending on the package. The exact list is on the Services page.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes. Maintenance plans run from $79 to $400 a month, with a minimum of 3 months. Details are on the Services page.

Can you work with businesses outside Lebanon?

Yes. Most of the work happens online, and we work with businesses across the region.
