Questions

Before you message us.

What happens in the free consultation?

A 30-minute video or in-person call. You leave with a clear scope and budget, whether or not you decide to work with us.

Do you work with businesses outside Beirut?

Yes. We build for businesses across Lebanon and the region, and most of the work happens online.

Will my site or menu work in Arabic?

Yes for menus, stores, booking pages and custom websites: CarteNex, ShopNex, Tymelo and our custom work support Arabic with right-to-left layouts. The LebaSouk POS screens are English for now.

How do payments work?

CarteNex is a monthly subscription. LebaSouk POS has no deposit and is paid once installed. Custom projects are billed in stages, which we agree in writing before we start.
